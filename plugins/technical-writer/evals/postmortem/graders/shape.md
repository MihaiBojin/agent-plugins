Genre shape: a postmortem has nine sections.

Pass only if the document has a heading for each of these, in this order. The heading text may differ when the meaning is the same:

1. Summary
2. Impact
3. Timeline
4. Causes
5. Detection
6. Response and recovery
7. What went well, what went badly, and where the team was lucky
8. Action items
9. Open questions

Fail if a section is missing, merged into another, or out of order. Name the section that decides it.
