# Status update

Reader: a lead or peer scanning for risk. They arrive asking what changed,
whether anything is at risk, and whether they are needed.

## Shape

An update has four parts, in about five lines:

1. Material changes.
2. Risks and blockers.
3. Needs: the decision, help or resources required, or "none".
4. Next.

## Rules

- Report the change, not an activity log.
- Put exceptions first (`bluf`).
- Measure progress: "8 of 12 endpoints migrated", never "good progress"
  (`quantify`).
- A status color or symbol always carries its word: "At risk", not an amber
  dot alone (`not_by_sight`).

Sources: the progress format and the CAN situation in the dialogue plugin;
practice.
