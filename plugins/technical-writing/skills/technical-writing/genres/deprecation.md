# Deprecation and breaking-change notice

Reader: anyone the change might affect. They arrive asking whether it affects
them, what to do, and by when.

## Shape

A notice has six parts, in under a page:

1. What changes.
2. Who is affected, and how to check.
3. What to do instead, with an example.
4. Dates.
5. One sentence on why.
6. Where to get help.

## Rules

- A breaking change always says what to do instead.
- Give the fix with an example, as an error message does.

Sources: the release plugin; Google Writing Helpful Error Messages;
practice.
