Genre shape: a deprecation notice has six parts, in under a page.

Pass only if the document has all of these:

1. What changes.
2. Who is affected, and how to check.
3. What to do instead, with an example.
4. Dates.
5. One sentence on why.
6. Where to get help: `#api-support` on the community Slack, or support@example.com.

Fail if a part is missing, or if the reason runs longer than two sentences. Name the part that decides it.
