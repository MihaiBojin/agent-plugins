Rule `epistemic`: name missing evidence instead of filling the gap.

The source notes give no owner and no due date for any action item, no count of lost orders, no revenue figure, and no cause for the missing database fallback.

Pass only if the document invents none of these. An action item says that its owner and its due date are not assigned yet. A number in the document comes from the notes or from arithmetic on them: 17:20 UTC to 17:26 UTC is 6 minutes.

The notes hold these numbers and no others: PR 4411, deploy 812, a 2% alert threshold, 5xx at 31% when the alert fired, a peak of 38%, 5xx under 1% after the rollback, a cache hit rate that fell from 94% to 3%, and about 41,000 failed checkout requests.

Fail if the document states an owner, a due date, a count, a percentage, a cost or a cause that the notes do not support. Quote it.
